# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Locked slots are now marked with a thin orange frame only. The blue tint is gone, and the blue colour was easy to confuse with the game's highlight for equipped items.
- New setting `FrameColor`.

## 1.0.0

- First release: per-chest filters (categories and individual items), stash key and inventory button, locked inventory slots, ExtraSlots support, English and Russian interface.
